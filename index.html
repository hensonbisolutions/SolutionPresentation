Executive Summary

The proposed solution introduces an AI-assisted staffing intelligence platform designed to improve workforce visibility, staffing quality, mobilisation speed, and allocation governance across Forward Deployed Engineering teams.


The platform combines structured workforce analytics, explainable recommendation logic, AI-assisted insight generation, and operational dashboards into a scalable enterprise solution.

Current Challenges

Fragmented Data

Workforce information distributed across multiple systems.

Limited Visibility

Lack of consolidated view across skills, certifications, and availability.

Slow Mobilisation

Manual staffing processes reduce operational agility.

Inconsistent Decisions

Staffing decisions dependent on individual knowledge.
